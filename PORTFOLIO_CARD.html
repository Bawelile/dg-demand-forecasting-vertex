Store-Item Demand Forecasting on Vertex AI
The full ML lifecycle, from raw data to a monitored model, with a gate that only ships a better forecast

An end-to-end system that forecasts 14 days of unit sales for every store and item in a dollar-store chain. A simulator builds two years of daily sales with SNAP and paycheck timing, rural and urban stores, promotions, and stockouts. The pipeline validates the data, builds leak-free features (proven by a unit test), trains XGBoost on a time-based split, and promotes a model only if it beats a seasonal-naive baseline and the live champion. Daily WAPE is 52.9% against 73.7% for the baseline, within 0.8 points of a perfect model; weekly WAPE is 24.2%, cutting forecast-error cost by about $60K in four weeks. Nightly forecasts carry their top three SHAP reasons. In a drift test, region-level monitoring caught a 46% over-forecast that the overall average hid. It all compiles to two Vertex AI pipelines: weekly training and daily monitoring.
